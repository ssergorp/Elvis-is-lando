The films that were made, I had to sort of figure out who everybody was, where they came from, how they got to be where they were, and what the dynamic relationships were between everybody. I. I had to sort of figure out the drama, but based in mythology. Prince and noble folk. It was very real but in a sense, just fantasy in space. This grand opera. Larger than life villains. Emperors. Wizards. It was very theatrical.

I designed it that way.

I needed someone who understood that. From the inside out.

I went to Joseph Campbell. And he said, "Mythology isn't just stories we tell to explain the unexplainable. Mythology is our corpus. Mythology is our body. It comes from something real. Somewhere real out there. Someone real in here."
And he pointed to my heart. And I thought back to my youth. And… I thought about growing up with Rock n Roll.

There was only one man I needed to talk to.
